# System Usability Scale (SUS) worksheet

Use this worksheet for the planned SUS round after the participant has
completed the agreed usability tasks. Administer the authorized, validated
10-item SUS wording unchanged; this worksheet intentionally provides answer
slots rather than reprinting the questionnaire. Do not paraphrase the SUS
items, change their order, or substitute task-specific questions.

## Score calculation

Calculate a score only when all ten responses are present and valid.

1. For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9), contribution = response − 1.
2. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10), contribution = 5 − response.
3. Add the ten contributions and multiply the sum by 2.5.

**SUS score = (sum of adjusted item contributions) × 2.5**

Record the resulting score from 0 to 100. It is a SUS score, **not a
percentage**. Do not interpret a single participant's score as a diagnosis or
as a pass/fail result.

| Scoring field | Value |
|---|---:|
| Odd-item adjusted subtotal (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) | |
| Even-item adjusted subtotal (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) | |
| Total adjusted contribution (0–40) | |
| SUS score (total × 2.5; 0–100) | |

If any item is unanswered or invalid, mark the SUS score **Incomplete**. Do not
silently impute a response. Ask the participant to complete a skipped item
only if they are comfortable; they may decline.
